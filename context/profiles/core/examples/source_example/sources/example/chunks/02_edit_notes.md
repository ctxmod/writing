Editing-pass notes over the retention-notice memo draft.

1. Opening order. The memo states the slip before the recovery plan;
   reverse the two sentences so the reader meets the path forward
   first and the cause second. The governing point is "lands
   tomorrow", not "we lost a day".

2. Evidence discipline. "Both numbers appear in the source policy"
   asserts without pointing. Name the section and clause the two
   readings come from, or the dispute will simply reopen at review.

3. The unblocked claim. "No downstream work is blocked" is the
   sentence the recipients care most about, and it currently sits
   last. Give it its own short paragraph and keep it there, but say
   what "blocked" was checked against.

4. Subject line. "Recovery attached" promises an attachment the memo
   does not carry; either attach the revised draft or retitle the
   subject to what the memo actually contains.

5. Close. The memo ends on a schedule sentence with no named owner
   for the confirmation of the notice period. Add the owner and the
   deadline: who confirms the period, by when.
