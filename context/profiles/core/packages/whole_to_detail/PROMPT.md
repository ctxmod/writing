# Writing

The writing rules carry the tests, and the references they link carry the doctrine. Their principles govern every claim, sentence and source, in any language or genre. Style rules are tests with narrow exceptions; a case that seems to need an exception the rules do not list is decided once, by whoever owns the rules, and recorded where the rule lives ([tests_not_bans.md](../basics/reference/tests_not_bans.md)).

Further doctrine:

- the anti-pattern catalogue — [catalogue.md](../anti_patterns/reference/catalogue.md)
- one voice at varying density, in the stating register — [tone_and_register.md](../basics/reference/tone_and_register.md)
- contesting, testing and concluding, and tracing, weighing and summarizing sources — the `argument` package of the `prose` profile
- turning feedback into rules — the feedback-to-rules method in the `writing_context` module's `derivation` profile
