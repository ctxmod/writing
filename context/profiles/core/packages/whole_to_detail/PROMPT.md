# Writing

Prose gives the frame before the detail it governs, at every level, and passes the five-question review test. Information is said now, made available later, moved to an appendix, or omitted, each by its test. Style rules are tests with narrow exceptions; a case that seems to need an exception the rules do not list is decided once, by whoever owns the rules, and recorded where the rule lives.

- tests, not bans — the authoring principle behind every style rule — [tests_not_bans.md](../basics/reference/tests_not_bans.md)
- the whole-to-detail model — [whole_to_detail.md](reference/whole_to_detail.md)
- the review test and the late-relevance symptom — [review_test.md](rules/review_test.md)
- the funnel and information placement — [reader_order.md](rules/reader_order.md)
- the anti-pattern catalogue — [catalogue.md](../anti_patterns/reference/catalogue.md)

The general principles govern every claim, sentence and source, in any language or genre:

- every clause adds information — [redundancy.md](../basics/reference/redundancy.md)
- precise terms and specific claims about technology — [diction.md](../basics/reference/diction.md)
- one voice at varying density, in the stating register — [tone_and_register.md](../basics/reference/tone_and_register.md)
- qualities shown through evidence, defensible claims — [show_qualities.md](../claims/rules/show_qualities.md)
- the general claims-and-evidence doctrine — [claims_and_evidence.md](../claims/reference/claims_and_evidence.md)
- contesting, testing and concluding, and tracing, weighing and summarizing sources — the `argument` package of the `prose` profile
