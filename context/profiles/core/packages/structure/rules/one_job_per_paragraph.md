# One Job per Paragraph

A paragraph makes one move: states one topic, develops it, and stops
when the next topic starts. The topic sentence goes first, states the
move plainly, and the remaining sentences either support it or get
cut.

## Rationale

Paragraphs are the reader's breathing units — a paragraph break
promises "you may pause here; nothing in the next block continues
this thought". When a second topic enters a paragraph, that promise
breaks: skimmers miss the second topic, and careful readers lose the
place. The fix is mechanical and cheap: whenever a sentence stops
being about the topic sentence's subject, start a new paragraph —
even a two-sentence paragraph is a paragraph. The mirror error is the
one-sentence paragraph habit, which flattens emphasis; a paragraph
break is an emphasis signal, and spending it on every sentence spends
it on nothing.
