# Answer First

Lead with the governing point: the conclusion, the answer, or the
request stands in the first sentence or two, and the support,
qualification, and background follow it. The reader who stops after
one paragraph should leave with the document's main claim intact.

As a test, move the last sentence of the first draft's opening paragraph to the front; that sentence is usually the document you meant to write. Genuine narrative that depends on suspense is the one exception, and it must be chosen, not defaulted into.
