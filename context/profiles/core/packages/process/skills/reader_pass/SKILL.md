description: >-
  Revision procedure for an outward draft. It names the reader, reads
  the draft once as that reader, cuts what tells them nothing, checks
  that the author stands behind every sentence, runs the machine-tell
  tests and the genre's own checks, and stops when the tests pass. Use it
  on any letter, message, application, page, document or essay before
  it is reported done, and when asked to make a draft sound less
  generic or less machine-written.

# Reader pass

Before revising an outward draft for its named reader, read [reader_pass.md](../../reference/reader_pass.md) and follow it step by step. It holds the inputs, the seven steps from naming the reader to stopping, and the output.
