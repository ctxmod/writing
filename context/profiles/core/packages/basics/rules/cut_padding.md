# Cut Padding

Delete every sentence that announces, warms up, or apologizes for the writing instead of doing it ("It is important to note that", "As you may already know", "I just wanted to quickly"). Cut single-word fillers (`very`, `really`, `actually`, `basically`, `quite`) unless the intensity does measurable work. The test is to mark the first seven words of every paragraph; anything there that carries no information is a candidate. A clause that restates the preceding clause in other words is never acceptable. Keep the more concrete formulation and cut the other ([redundancy.md](../reference/redundancy.md)).
