# Clarity Principles

Clear writing is writing whose reader can reconstruct the events and
the reasoning without asking the author. Three levers produce most of
it: concrete subjects, strong verbs, and visible actors.

## Concrete subjects

Make the subject of each sentence the thing the sentence is about.
Abstractions as subjects ("the implementation of the feature",
"the utilization of the tool") force the reader to hold an action in
suspense; a concrete subject delivers it up front
("we implemented the feature", "the team used the tool"). When a
sentence's subject is a nominalization — an action frozen into a noun
(`decision`, `evaluation`, `discussion`) — thaw it back into a verb:
"we decided", "they evaluated", "the group discussed".

## Strong verbs

Verbs carry the energy of a sentence, and weak verbs spend it. Prefer
verbs that state the action (`cut`, `verify`, `ship`, `collapse`) over
forms of "be" and "have" propping up a buried action
("there is a requirement that we verify" → "we must verify"). A strong
verb also absorbs adverbs: "walked quickly" becomes "hurried";
"very large" becomes "vast" when the size matters.

## Visible actors

Every action needs an actor the reader can see. "Mistakes were made"
and "it was decided" are clarity failures before they are voice
failures: no one can be questioned, corrected, or thanked. Name the
actor even when the name is awkward ("the operator", "the release
process", "the 2019 policy") — an awkward true actor beats a smooth
false one.

## Before and after

- Before: "It was determined that utilization of the legacy pipeline
  should be discontinued." After: "The platform team decided to retire
  the legacy pipeline."
- Before: "There were three test failures observed in the nightly
  run." After: "The nightly run failed three tests."
- Before: "A decision was made by the committee to postpone."
  After: "The committee postponed."

Passive voice stays available for its three honest uses — unknown,
irrelevant, or deliberately de-emphasized actor — but those are
choices, not defaults.
