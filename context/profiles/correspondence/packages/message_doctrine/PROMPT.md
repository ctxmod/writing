# Correspondence

Messages, replies and answers to posed questions follow the base writing rule and its references in full; this rule adds the genre layer. The first sentence gives the recipient the frame for their decision, content and relationship are decided before any sentence is worded, and the message stops when the step it asks for is clear. Sales outreach applies this doctrine through the `sales_outbound` profile of this module, which extends this one and governs sales outbound; a conflict between the two goes to whoever owns the writing rules. Where a sales message applies a rule here differently, the sales application is stated there and governs the sales message. A cover letter written as an email body follows the `job_application` profile; the short answer box in an application form follows the application-answers rule below, and the letter guidance sets only the length rule such a box imposes.
