# Correspondence

This profile adds the genre layer for messages, replies and answers to posed questions, on top of the writing core. The first sentence gives the recipient the frame for their decision, content and relationship are decided before any sentence is worded, and the message stops when the step it asks for is clear. The `sales_outbound` profile extends this one and governs sales outbound; where a sales message applies a rule here differently, its application there governs, and a conflict between the two goes to whoever owns the writing rules. A cover letter written as an email body follows the `job_application` profile; the short answer box in an application form follows the application-answers rule.

- the message doctrine: the motivated next step, the recipient's decision first, information before words and its four placements, information order, the actual relationship, precision about what is known, peer stance and recipient control, credibility, short without skimpy, the working order — [message_doctrine.md](reference/message_doctrine.md)
- messages, replies and personal contacts: density, the opening and the intent-stating subject line, saying only what serves the message, answering the answer, concrete times, the warm-referral register and its exclamation greeting — [messages.md](../replies/reference/messages.md)
- relational messages and machine voice: thanks, apology, condolence and congratulation written by the sender and naming the specific thing, and the everyday email habits that read as generated — [relational_messages.md](reference/relational_messages.md)
