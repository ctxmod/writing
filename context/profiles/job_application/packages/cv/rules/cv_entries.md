# CV entries

Each entry opens with what was owned and delivered, then says how, never with the technology, the team or the sector. Entries read as connected prose; bullets are kept for genuinely list-like detail such as a stack or discrete deliverables, introduced by a sentence that says what the list is. Use past tense for finished roles and present tense for the current one, never both within one role. A quality adjective appears only with its evidence beside it, a figure only where an honest measure exists, and a competence list holds only what survives three follow-up questions at an interview. Hold one responsibilities heading per CV, "Main Responsibilities" by preference.
