# CV entries

A CV entry is read as an inventory of what was owned and delivered, and the claims discipline it owes — selection by evidenced capability, figures with substantiation, the stable fact base — is in [claims_in_applications.md](../../letters/reference/claims_in_applications.md). This file sets the entry-level rules.

## What leads an entry

Each entry opens with what was owned and delivered, then says how. An entry that opens with the technology, the team or the sector gives the reader the packaging before the content, and the owner-and-delivered lead is what a screener scanning entries extracts anyway.

## Prose, with lists where lists belong

Entries read as connected prose. Bullets are kept for genuinely list-like detail — a technology stack, discrete deliverables — with the sentence before the list saying what the list is, per entries 1 to 3 of [catalogue.md](../../../../core/packages/anti_patterns/reference/catalogue.md). Reasoning, responsibility and causality stay in paragraphs, where the connections between the parts can be stated. The genre default is first-person flowing prose; a verb-initial bullet style, where a house uses one, still passes only where the content genuinely is a list.

## Tense

Past tense for finished roles; present tense for the current one. The test is whether the role is over, and an entry that mixes the two within one role leaves the reader dating the work by guesswork.

## Qualities and figures

Qualities are conveyed through the work itself, and a quality adjective appears only with its evidence right beside it, never stacked, per [show_qualities.md](../../../../core/packages/claims/rules/show_qualities.md). A figure is used where an honest measure exists; where none does, scope and outcome are stated in words, per the figures rule in [claims_in_applications.md](../../letters/reference/claims_in_applications.md).

An entry, finished role, past tense, first person (illustrative):

> I led the migration of a legacy batch system to a service architecture, designed the replacement, set up its pipeline and database, and ran the deployment together with a team that was skeptical at the start.

## Competence lists

A competence list contains only what survives three follow-up questions at an interview, per [show_qualities.md](../../../../core/packages/claims/rules/show_qualities.md) on defensible entries. An item that would be hedged in the room is cut from the page.

## The responsibilities heading

A heading over an entry's responsibilities reads "Main Responsibilities"; the Swedish form is in `svenska_ansokningar` (module `writing_sv`, profile `jobbansokan`, package `ansokan`). Hold one form per CV; "Main Responsibilities" is the recommended form and "Key Responsibilities" the common variant it replaces.
