# Job applications

Job applications follow the base writing rule and its references in full; this rule adds the genre layer. The letter is carried by motivation — why the applicant wants to be there, not why the applicant qualifies — and opens with it, every example is selected by the capability the reader needs evidence of, and the facts behind the document stay invariant across every adaptation of it.

- claims and evidence in letters and CVs: importance-led evidence, delimited transfer, usage-level results, responsibility weighting, one job per example, working under rules, the stable fact base, motivation claims, the figures and years rulings, the deletion test and the review passes — [claims_in_applications.md](reference/claims_in_applications.md)
- the cover letter: motivation carries the letter and the forbidden CV-retelling openings, naming an area is not a reason, humility toward the role, precise and proportionate background, the pride anchor of a short application email, the examples and the one connection, the close, voice, heading and frames, language choice — [cover_letter.md](reference/cover_letter.md)
- authorship in applications: the letter's value is the applicant's own reasoning, assistance that cleans the prose and leaves the substance, the interview test, the tells screeners meet most often, employer policies on assisted applications — [authorship_in_applications.md](reference/authorship_in_applications.md)
- svenska jobbansökningar: motivationen bär brevet, ödmjukhet mot rollen, rubrik och ram, stoltheten som ankare, tempus, Huvudansvar, teknikstödlista, gardering, språkval — `svenska_ansokningar` (module `writing_sv`, profile `jobbansokan`, package `ansokan`)
