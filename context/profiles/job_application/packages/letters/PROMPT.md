# Job applications

Job applications follow the base writing rule and its references in full; this rule adds the genre layer. The letter is carried by motivation — why the applicant wants to be there, not why the applicant qualifies — and opens with it, every example is selected by the capability the reader needs evidence of, and the facts behind the document stay invariant across every adaptation of it.

- claims and evidence in letters and CVs: importance-led evidence, delimited transfer, usage-level results, responsibility weighting, one job per example, working under rules, the stable fact base, motivation claims, the figures and years rulings, the deletion test and the review passes — <ctxmod_path path="ctx:github.com/ctxmod/writing/context::shared::letters@job_application/reference/claims_in_applications.md" />
- the cover letter: motivation carries the letter and the forbidden CV-retelling openings, naming an area is not a reason, humility toward the role, precise and proportionate background, the pride anchor of a short application email, the examples and the one connection, the close, voice, heading and frames, language choice — <ctxmod_path path="ctx:github.com/ctxmod/writing/context::shared::letters@job_application/reference/cover_letter.md" />
- CV entries: the owned-and-delivered lead, prose with lists where lists belong, tense, qualities, competence lists, the responsibilities heading — <ctxmod_path path="ctx:github.com/ctxmod/writing/context::shared::cv@job_application/rules/cv_entries.md" />
- svenska jobbansökningar: motivationen bär brevet, ödmjukhet mot rollen, rubrik och ram, stoltheten som ankare, tempus, Huvudansvar, teknikstödlista, gardering, språkval — `svenska_ansokningar` (module `writing_sv`, profile `jobbansokan`, package `ansokan`)
