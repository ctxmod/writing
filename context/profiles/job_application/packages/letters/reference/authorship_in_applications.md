# Authorship in applications

An application asks a screener to believe things about a person they have not met, and the letter is the one part of it that shows how the applicant thinks. The core `integrity` package holds the general rules on ownership and assistance ([author_integrity.md](../../../../core/packages/integrity/reference/author_integrity.md)). This file states what they mean for letters, interest texts and form answers, and names the tells screeners meet most often in them.

## The letter's value is the applicant's own reasoning

Written applications used to work partly as a costly signal, since a letter tailored to one job showed attention that cost the applicant something. On a large freelance platform, Galdin and Silbert (working paper, 2025) found that employers paid more for workers whose applications were customized to the job before LLMs, and stopped doing so after. A letter that anyone could have generated for any posting now tells the screener nothing about the applicant. Surface help is a different matter. In a field experiment, writing assistance that fixed spelling and grammar on resumes raised hiring by about 8 percent, and employers were not later disappointed (Wiles, Munyikwa and Horton, 2025). The evidence and its limits are in [why_machine_text_fails.md](../../../../core/packages/machine_tells/reference/why_machine_text_fails.md) § 3.

So help may clean the prose, and the substance is the applicant's. That covers the motivation, the choice of examples, the connection to this employer, and every claim. This is the application form of [author_integrity.md](../../../../core/packages/integrity/reference/author_integrity.md) § 6, and it matches what [cover_letter.md](cover_letter.md) already requires. There the motivation is true and anchored, a reason with no source goes to the applicant for confirmation, and nothing is invented.

## The interview test

Every sentence of a letter is one the applicant could say in the interview and expand on when asked. The test extends the three-follow-up-questions rule for list entries in [show_qualities.md](../../../../core/packages/claims/rules/show_qualities.md) to the whole letter. A sentence the applicant would struggle to discuss, such as a claim polished past what they did or a reason they would not give in their own words, goes. An interviewer who meets a candidate unable to stand behind their own letter discounts everything else in it.

## Tells screeners meet most often

The forms below are the machine tells of [tell_catalogue.md](../../../../core/packages/machine_tells/reference/tell_catalogue.md) as they appear in applications. Most already fail an existing rule of this profile, and the pointer names it:

- "I am excited to apply for the position of …" and "I am writing to express my interest in …" as the opening fail the motivation-first opening of [cover_letter.md](cover_letter.md) and the service-frame entry of the tell catalogue.
- "I am confident that my skills and experience make me an ideal candidate" is a CV-retelling claim and a self-label, and fails the forbidden openings of [cover_letter.md](cover_letter.md) and [show_qualities.md](../../../../core/packages/claims/rules/show_qualities.md).
- "Proven track record", "results-driven", "dynamic team", "fast-paced environment", "aligns perfectly with my passion for" are stock vocabulary and performed feeling (tell catalogue entries 1 and 14) and the enthusiasm adjectives [cover_letter.md](cover_letter.md) cuts.
- A letter that mirrors the advertisement's list back point by point is the match recital [cover_letter.md](cover_letter.md) rules out.
- "I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute to your continued success" is a service closer with an inflated-significance tail. The close in [cover_letter.md](cover_letter.md) states interest and mutuality in one plain sentence.
- Three adjectives for the applicant ("motivated, adaptable and detail-oriented") are a habitual triad of self-labels (anti-pattern catalogue entry 12).

## Policies on assisted applications

Some employers, schools and programmes state whether applicants may use AI tools for application material, or ask them to declare it. Such a statement is binding policy and is followed, per [disclosing_assistance.md](../../../../core/packages/integrity/reference/disclosing_assistance.md) § 1. Where nothing is stated, silence is allowed and a false statement is not (§ 3 there).
