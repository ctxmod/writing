# Replies and follow-up

Correspondence `messages.md` governs replies and threads for every message: a reply answers what was actually written, and a resolved thread is not pivoted to a new topic ([messages.md](../../../../correspondence/packages/replies/reference/messages.md)). Correspondence hands follow-up in a sales thread to this file. Two rules shape it: the fixed cadence for an unanswered first contact is kept, and follow-up for a reason is kept beside it.

## Answering a reply

When a reply resolves the hypothesis the first contact rested on, whether by confirming that the problem is handled or that it does not exist, the hypothesis is dropped, and the sender does not pivot automatically to another topic to keep the thread alive. When the reply makes the need clear enough, the next message moves to a concrete offer under [credibility_and_offers.md](../reference/credibility_and_offers.md) and stops gathering information without an end. After a positive reply, the next message asks for what is needed to judge a next step, and no more.

## A no ends the thread

An explicit no is a qualification outcome, never an objection to wear down. No follow-up follows a no or a request to stop, and the stop is honoured at once and permanently; how a no is recorded and enforced (contact blocks, suppression lists) belongs to the consuming repository's compliance and qualification mechanics. An agreed later time is respected as agreed. Silence is recorded as silence, and it is never read as irritation, hidden interest, or permission to escalate to another channel.

## How the two follow-up rules coexist

Two kinds of follow-up are allowed in a sales thread, and every follow-up belongs to one of them.

The cadence follow-up applies to one case: a first contact that has received no reply. One follow-up is sent after about 5–7 working days, in the same thread, shorter than the first message: a one-line nudge with a genuine out, for example "Following up in case this slipped — no worries either way, and I'll leave it there." After it, there is no second nudge. The thread is parked, and the relationship stays open for a future, genuinely new reason to write. The cadence sets both the timing and the ceiling: it permits this one nudge without a new reason, and nothing more on a timer. Sending no nudge at all is also legitimate, because silence can end a thread.

The reason follow-up applies at any point in any sales thread, including after the cadence follow-up has been sent and the thread parked. A follow-up is sent when there is an agreed callback, a relevant new fact, or a decision that is genuinely pending, and the message states that reason. A reason never comes from a date alone.

The test for a planned follow-up: is it the single cadence nudge on an unanswered first contact, or does it carry a stated reason the recipient would recognize as one? A follow-up that is neither fails, and so does any follow-up after a no. An automatic series of reminders fails by construction.

How a consuming repository records the basis of a follow-up, and whether a validator enforces it, is its mechanics; which follow-ups are allowed is decided here.
