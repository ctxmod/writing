# Replies and follow-up

A reply answers what was actually written, and a resolved thread is not pivoted to a new topic ([messages.md](../../../../correspondence/packages/replies/reference/messages.md)). When a reply resolves the hypothesis the first contact rested on, drop the hypothesis; when the need is clear, move to a concrete offer under [credibility_and_offers.md](../reference/credibility_and_offers.md); after a positive reply, ask only for what is needed to judge a next step. An explicit no or a request to stop ends the thread at once and permanently, an agreed later time is respected, and silence is recorded as silence, never read as interest or as permission to escalate to another channel.

Every follow-up in a sales thread is one of two kinds. The cadence follow-up is a single shorter nudge with a genuine out, in the same thread, about 5–7 working days after an unanswered first contact, and nothing more follows on a timer. The reason follow-up may come at any point, for an agreed callback, a relevant new fact or a genuinely pending decision, and states that reason; a date alone is never a reason. A planned follow-up that is neither fails, and so does any follow-up after a no and any automatic series of reminders.
