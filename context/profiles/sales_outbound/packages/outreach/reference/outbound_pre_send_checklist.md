# Pre-send checks for sales messages in full

The [outbound_pre_send_checks.md](../rules/outbound_pre_send_checks.md) rule states the additional hard stops, the hypothesis check and the control question in brief, and requires the sales readings of the checks. This file holds them in full.

A sales message passes the correspondence pre-send checks in full before it leaves: the hard stops, the checklist, the interchangeability, forwarding and answer-cost tests, and the final control question ([pre_send_checks.md](../../../../correspondence/packages/message_doctrine/rules/pre_send_checks.md)). This file adds what the sales situation adds. The consuming repository's compliance controls (marketing-law and personal-data checks, contact-rule checks, suppression lists, send approval) run separately, and nothing here replaces them.

## Additional hard stops

The message does not leave while any of these holds:

- The recipient has declined contact, or a contact block applies.
- Suitability, authority, or a required contact-rule check is missing.
- The message promises a demo, report or analysis that does not exist.
- The message implies an existing relationship, a referral endorsement or a customer history that is not true.

## Sales readings of the checks

The checklist item on tone reads, for a sales message: the tone implies neither insight the sender does not have, nor superiority, nor social obligation, and the whole text does not add up to selling even where each sentence passes on its own.

The checklist item on consistency reads: the message matches the sender's website, the offer as registered, and actual capacity.

The forwarding test reads: is the content still correct and defensible when the budget owner, the IT manager or a competing supplier reads it?

The interchangeability test applies to the hook: swap the recipient's name and company, and a hook that still reads correctly is decoration, per the swap control in [first_contact.md](first_contact.md).

## Hypotheses stay hypotheses

Any statement in the draft about what the recipient feels, wants or will react to is checked against principle 7 of [outbound_principles.md](outbound_principles.md): it may have steered a wording choice as a hypothesis, and it does not appear in the message as fact.

## The control question

The final question is the correspondence one, asked of the commercial step: has the recipient been given sufficient, correct and relevant reasons for exactly the step this message asks for? Whether the message sounds convincing is beside it.
