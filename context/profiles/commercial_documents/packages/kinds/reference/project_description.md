# Project description

A project description lets the client judge how the work will reach the goal and follow it while it runs. Its spine is the chain the base model gives as its own example of stepwise precision, quoted from [whole_to_detail.md](../../../../core/packages/whole_to_detail/reference/whole_to_detail.md):

> The project's goal → the chosen strategy → the work packages' responsibilities → concrete activities → deliveries and acceptance criteria.

Each level explains the role of the next, so the document's order follows the chain and no level is skipped. The design of the project description decides how the levels map onto parts, pages and furniture; this file sets what each level must give the reader, on top of [commercial_document_principles.md](../../principles/reference/commercial_document_principles.md) and [appendix_rule.md](../../principles/rules/appendix_rule.md).

## Goal

The goal opens the document. It states the outcome the client wants, in the client's terms, and the delimitation of the project, with the model's document-opening example in [commercial_document_principles.md](../../principles/reference/commercial_document_principles.md) as the pattern. A goal stated as the vendor's activity ("we will build …") leaves the reader without the criterion every later level is judged against; the goal states what will be different for the client when the project is done.

## Strategy

The strategy states the chosen approach and why it reaches the goal, before any work package appears. It is the level most often skipped, and its absence is what makes a list of work packages read as an inventory. Where the reader would weigh an alternative, the strategy names the criterion that decided between them, per the model's rule that criteria come before the comparison they govern. A contrast with a rejected approach stays only where the reader actually holds that approach as the default, per entry 4 of [catalogue.md](../../../../core/packages/anti_patterns/reference/catalogue.md).

## Work packages and activities

Each work package opens with its responsibility: what part of the strategy it carries and what it hands to the next. The activities follow as the concrete steps of that responsibility. When packages share a structure, the shared structure shows in their identical order and headings; a sentence announcing that structure is a meta statement under catalogue entry 5 and is omitted.

Work packages are a genuine list of parallel parts and may be introduced together by a sentence that states what unites them — phases of one process, or parts with separate responsibilities — before each is developed. The relation is stated before the parts are enumerated, per the model's "relations before enumeration".

## Deliveries and acceptance criteria

Each work package ends in what it delivers and how the delivery is accepted. An acceptance criterion is checkable: the client can tell from the criterion alone whether the delivery meets it. Acceptance criteria are a genuine list and are set as one where there are several.

## Price-free

A project description carries no price, no rate and no commercial terms; all commercial content lives in the offer. The link between the two documents runs from the offer only: the offer points to the project description for how the work is done, and the project description points to its own sections and appendices and never to the offer, per § References between documents in [commercial_document_principles.md](../../principles/reference/commercial_document_principles.md). Where the chain touches a commercial matter, such as the decision that starts the project, it states the step without naming the offer or any figure. This keeps each figure stated once, per the price test in the same file, and lets the project description be read and shared on its own.

## Appendices

Method detail, technical specifications and inventories that the chain does not need go to named appendix sub-sections, per [appendix_rule.md](../../principles/rules/appendix_rule.md). A reader who follows the chain from goal to acceptance in the main text understands the whole project.
